Title

Sacred Heart of Jesus

Artist

Artiggio Store

Medium

Painting - Oil On Canvas

Description

Sacred Heart of Jesus is a classic devotional image that presents Christ in a direct and compassionate frontal pose, raising one hand in blessing while indicating the radiant heart at the center of the composition. The glowing halo, luminous golden background, and warm red and white garments create an atmosphere of mercy, holiness, and spiritual comfort. The flaming heart, encircled by thorns and marked with a cross, is rendered as the central symbol of divine love and sacrifice, giving the image both theological depth and strong emotional presence. This work is especially suited to collectors of Catholic art, prayer room decor, and sacred imagery that brings a sense of peace, reverence, and timeless Christian devotion into the home.
