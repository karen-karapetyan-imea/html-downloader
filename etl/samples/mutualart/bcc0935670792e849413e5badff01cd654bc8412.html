High and low – a collection of Norwegian and international children's furniture

Drammens Museum

 Drammen | Norway
28 Oct, 2026 - 03 Jan, 2027
  • Outlook
  • Google Calendar
  • iCalendar

The 20th century has been described as the century of the child because the needs of the little ones began to be given priority, both literally and in an extended sense when it came to the correct sitting position and height. Ergonomics and functionality provided new opportunities to create furniture for children that did not just consist of scaled-down chairs to fit into the adult world, or as part of a designer's series. Developments followed in the footsteps of modernism's new materials and production techniques, but innovative seating devices for children already appeared early in the 20th century, for example in the form of chairs with multiple functions in one and the same piece of furniture. Worldwide, the diversity in the production of children's furniture has been great, but even the more unknown Norwegian examples follow international trends with determination and the ability to think new things.

The exhibition High and low embraces different approaches to the design of children's furniture throughout the 20th century. The history of the Norwegian production of fashionable as well as innovative children's furniture is both highlighted and placed in an international context. The majority of the chairs in this collection are rarely seen in children's rooms around the world today. One goal of the exhibition is to shed light on lesser-known practitioners from the history of design, and through this both surprise and create recognition.

The selection of furniture is based on many years of fundraising and collaboration between collector Thomas Flor and the design gallery Utopia Retro Modern.
