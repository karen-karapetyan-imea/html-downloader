Our latest Modern British & 20th Century Art sale is led by three important collections: A Distinguished British Private Collection (lots 1-9) including Scottish Colourists Samuel John Peploe and George Leslie Hunter; The Estate of the Late Trevor Coldrey (lot 16-33) including Julian Trevelyan; Works from the Collection of Mrs Eileen Carter (lot 10-15) including Eliot Hodgkin and Winifred Nicholson. We also have a wide range of works by Bloomsbury artists (lot 41-47), including Duncan Grant, Edward Le Bas and a portrait of the artist's son Quentin Bell by Vanessa Bell. Other early highlights include a monumental sculpture by Leslie Thornton (lot 183). Contemporary highlights include works by Helen Chadwick (lot 441), Prunella Clough (lot 249) and Gillian Ayres (lot 253). We also have a strong collection of Modern & Contemporary African Art (lots 519-530) including El Anatsui and Uche Okeke.
